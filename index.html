What Scout does

A customer-interview partner for Shopify founders with no research team.

After a shopper visits or buys, Scout interviews them by voice. It already knows what they did on the store, so it asks the right question, and brings the owner the reason in the shopper's own words. The owner decides what to do with it.

An interview, in short · candle store

ScoutYou looked at the Fig & Cedar candle three times. What made you hold off?
ShopperHonestly, the price, I think.
ScoutFair. What would have made it feel worth it?
ShopperIn the photo the jar looked tiny. I thought it was a little votive.

What the owner gets

"The jar looks tiny in the photo." Not price.

A survey would have said "price". The second question found the real reason, and the owner knows exactly what to fix. That's the story told in "The Dip", the ad above.
